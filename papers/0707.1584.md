# On the K^+D Interaction at Low Energies

> The Kd reactions are considered in the impulse approximation with NN final-state interactions (NN FSI) taken into account. The realistic parameters for the KN phase shifts are used. The "quasi-elastic" energy region, in which the elementary KN interaction is predominantly elastic, is considered. The theoretical predictions are compared with the data on the K^+d->K^+pn, K^+d->K^0pp, K^+d->K^+d and K^+d total cross sections. The NN FSI effect in the reaction K^+d->K^+pn has been found to be large. The predictions for the Kd cross sections are also given for slow kaons, produced from phi(1020) decays, as the functions of the isoscalar KN scattering length a_0. These predictions can be used to extract the value of a_0 from the data.

## Key claim

The paper demonstrates that the NN final-state interaction (FSI) effect in the reaction K⁺d → K⁺pn is large, but this conclusion is hedged by the approximation that only s-wave KN scattering is used in the FSI calculation, meaning the NN FSI is treated only in the triplet ³S₁ state and is absent in the K⁺d → K⁰pp reaction due to the Pauli principle.

## Method

The authors calculate K⁺d cross sections in the impulse approximation using pole diagrams for the break-up reactions K⁺d → K⁺pn and K⁺d → K⁰pp, plus a diagram for NN final-state interaction (FSI) in the triplet state. The KN scattering amplitudes include only s- and p-wave contributions from the phase-shift parameters of Gibbs (2005), and the deuteron wave function from the Bonn potential (full model) is used. The elastic K⁺d → K⁺d cross section is computed in a single-scattering approximation with form factors derived from the deuteron wave function.

## Key numbers

- The s-wave KN scattering lengths from Gibbs (2005) are a₁ = -0.308 fm and a₀ = -0.1048 fm; from Gibbs (1999) they are a₁ = -0.328 fm and a₀ = -0.06 fm.
- At kaon laboratory momentum p_LAB = 100 MeV/c, the elastic K⁺d cross section is 34.4 mb, the inelastic (K⁺pn + K⁺pp) cross section is 0.9 mb with NN FSI and 9.7 mb without, and the total cross section is 35.3 mb with NN FSI and 44.1 mb without.
- The NN scattering parameters used are a_NN^(0) = -5.4 fm, r₀ = 1.7 fm, and β ≈ 240 MeV for the triplet ³S₁ state.
- Predictions are given at p_LAB = 127 MeV/c (kaon momentum from φ(1020) decay) as functions of a₀, for two fixed values of a₁ = -0.328 fm and a₁ = -0.308 fm.

## Context

This work extends the impulse-approximation calculations of K⁺d reactions by Gibbs (1999) and Sibirtsev (2005), who analyzed K⁺d data in the quasi-elastic region (p_LAB < 0.8 GeV/c) using the Jülich model for KN amplitudes. The paper improves upon Gibbs (1999) by including the NN FSI correction and using realistic KN phase shifts from Gibbs (2005), and it provides predictions for extracting the isoscalar KN scattering length a₀ from future experiments at the DAΦNE accelerator using kaons from φ(1020) decays. The results enable a more precise determination of a₀, which is needed to constrain the isospin-0 KN interaction and to test for possible exotic states such as the Θ⁺ pentaquark.
