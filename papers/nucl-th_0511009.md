# Refractive Distortions of Two-Particle Correlations

> Using optical model calculations it has recently been shown that refractive phenomena from the collective mean field can significantly alter the sizes inferred from two-pion correlations. We demonstrate that such effects can be accounted for in classical calculations if mean field effects are included.

## Key claim

Refractive distortions from the collective mean field, previously shown to significantly alter two-pion correlation size inferences in optical model calculations, can be accounted for in classical calculations if mean field effects are included.

## Method

The author uses optical model calculations to demonstrate refractive phenomena from the collective mean field, then shows that these effects can be reproduced in classical calculations by incorporating mean field effects. The work compares quantum optical model results with classical calculations that include the mean field.
