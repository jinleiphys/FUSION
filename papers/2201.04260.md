# Large enhancement of total reaction cross sections at the edge of the island of inversion in Ti, Cr, and Fe isotopes

> A systematic analysis of nuclear deformation is made for neutron-rich Ti, Cr, and Fe isotopes to explore the nuclear structure in the island of inversion near N = 40, where strong nuclear deformation is predicted. The nuclear ground states are obtained by the Skyrme Hartree-Fock method in three-dimensional coordinate space, which properly describes any nuclear shape. Three types of Skyrme interactions are employed to generate various deformed states in its isotopic chain. We find that in the island of inversion the occupation of highly elongated intruder orbits induces not only large quadrupole deformation but also large hexadecapole deformation. This appears as a sizable enhancement of the nuclear matter radius, showing the characteristic shell effect of the density profile near the nuclear surface. We show that the edge of the island of inversion, where the intruder orbit starts being occupied, can be determined by measuring the enhancement of the total reaction cross section at high incident energy. The possibility of constraining the hexadecapole deformation by a measurement of the total reaction cross sections is discussed.

## Key claim

The paper claims that the edge of the island of inversion near N=40 in Ti, Cr, and Fe isotopes can be determined by measuring the enhancement of the total reaction cross section at high incident energy, but this claim is conditional on the specific Skyrme interaction used, the SkM* interaction predicts a sudden increase at N=34, while SLy4 and SkI3 predict the increase at N=42, making the experimental identification dependent on which theoretical model is correct.

## Method

The authors performed Skyrme Hartree-Fock calculations in three-dimensional coordinate space to obtain ground-state density distributions for neutron-rich Ti, Cr, and Fe isotopes, using three different Skyrme interactions (SkM*, SLy4, and SkI3). They then computed total reaction cross sections on a carbon target at 240 MeV/nucleon using the Glauber model with the nucleon-target formalism, which incorporates multiple scattering effects without adjustable parameters. The deformation parameters β₂ and β₄ were extracted from the intrinsic density distributions, and the cumulative single-particle hexadecapole moments were analyzed to identify the role of intruder orbitals.

- The 0f₅/₂-0g₉/₂ level spacings in spherical ⁹⁰Zr are 3.14 MeV (SkM*), 4.99 MeV (SLy4), and 7.09 MeV (SkI3)
- The cross section differences in the neutron range 34 < N < 40 are at most more than 2%, which can be distinguished by measurement as its uncertainty is typically less than 1%
- For ⁶²Cr, the cumulative ⟨Q₄₀⟩ values at neutron/proton numbers 10, 24, and 36 are 48.0, 94.9, and 166.3 fm⁴ respectively
- The β₄ value increases from N=18 to 20: from β₄=0.03 to 0.22 for Ne, and from β₄=0.00 to 0.15 for Mg with the SkM* interaction
- The approximate harmonic-oscillator expression gives ⟨nn0|Q₄₀|nn0⟩ = 43, 130, and 260 fm⁴ for n=2, 3, and 4 respectively with ℏω=40A⁻¹/³ MeV and A=62

## Context

This work extends the methodology previously applied to the island of inversion near N=20 (Takechi 2012, Minomo 2011, Horiuchi 2012) to the N=40 region, where large-scale shell-model calculations by Lenzi (2010) and experimental indications by Santamaria (2015) have explored the territory of the island. The paper enables future experimental measurements of total reaction cross sections for Ti, Cr, and Fe isotopes to determine the location of the island of inversion edge and potentially constrain hexadecapole deformation, which has been difficult to extract from electric quadrupole transition strengths due to model dependence.
