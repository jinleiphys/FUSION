# Vector Meson Photoproduction with an Effective Lagrangian in the Quark Model

> A quark model approach to the photoproduction of vector mesons off nucleons is proposed. Its starting point is an effective Lagrangian of the interaction between the vector meson and the quarks inside the baryon, which generates the non-diffractive s- and u- channel resonance contributions. Additional t-channel $π^0$ and $σ$ exchanges are included for the $ω$ and $ρ^0$ production respectively to account for the large diffractive behavior in the small $t$ region as suggested by Friman and Soyeur. The numerical results are presented for the $ω$ and $ρ$ productions in four isospin channels with the same set of parameters, and they are in good agreement with the available data not only in $ω$ and $ρ^0$ productions but also in the charged $ρ$ productions where the additional t-channel $σ$ exchange does not contribute so that it provides an important test to this approach. The investigation is also extended to the $ϕ$ photoproduction, and the initial results show that the non-diffractive behavior of the $ϕ$ productions in the large $t$ region can be described by the s- and u- channel contributions with significantly smaller coupling constants, which is consistent with the findings in the similar studies in the QHD framework. The numerical investigation has also shown that polarization observables are essential for identifying so-called "missing resonances".

## Key claim

The paper proposes a quark model approach to vector meson photoproduction that uses an effective Lagrangian for the quark-meson interaction, but the agreement with data is achieved only after adding additional t-channel π⁰ and σ exchanges for ω and ρ⁰ production respectively, and the model's success is explicitly conditional on the large-t region where non-diffractive contributions dominate.

## Method

The authors construct an effective Lagrangian for the interaction between vector mesons and quarks inside baryons, then compute s-channel, u-channel, and t-channel transition amplitudes in a nonrelativistic quark model with harmonic oscillator basis. S-channel resonances below 2 GeV are included explicitly while those above 2 GeV are treated as degenerate; u-channel contributions are divided into n=0 states (spin 1/2 and spin 3/2 treated separately) and degenerate excited states. Additional t-channel π⁰ exchange is added for ω production and σ exchange for ρ⁰ production to account for diffractive behavior at small t, following Friman and Soyeur.

## Key numbers

- Threshold photon energy for ω and ρ photoproduction: E_thres_γ ≈ 1.11 GeV
- Threshold photon energy for φ photoproduction: E_thres_γ ≈ 1.57 GeV
- The model includes s-channel resonances explicitly below 2 GeV, with resonances above 2 GeV treated as degenerate
- The harmonic oscillator parameter α² is used throughout, related to the harmonic oscillator strength (no numerical value given in the text)
- The φNN coupling constants are found to be "small but significant" compared to ωNN or ρNN couplings (no numerical values provided)

## Context

This work extends the quark model approach previously developed for pseudoscalar meson photoproduction (Zhao, Li, Bennhold, pseudoscalar studies) to vector meson photoproduction, addressing the puzzle that nonrelativistic quark models predict a richer resonance spectrum than observed in πN scattering. The approach enables identification of so-called "missing resonances" through polarization observables, and the φ photoproduction results are consistent with findings in the quantum hadrodynamical (QHD) framework of Williams (1997) and with OZI rule violation studies.
