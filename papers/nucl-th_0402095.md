# e+e- pair production from nucleon targets in the resonance region

> We present consistent theoretical descriptions of the $πN \to e^+e^- N$ and $γN \to e^+e^- N$ reactions on proton and neutron targets for total center of mass energies $\sqrt s$ ranging between 1.50 GeV and 1.75 GeV. These reactions are complementary to study the coupling of low-lying baryon resonances to vector meson-nucleon channels. We show in particular how the resonant structure of the amplitudes for both processes generates specific and large quantum interferences between $ρ$- and $ω$-meson decays into $e^+e^-$ pairs. Data on the $πN \to e^+e^- N$ and $γN \to e^+e^- N$ reactions are expected in the near future from the HADES program at GSI and from dilepton studies with CLAS at JLab.

## Key claim

The paper presents a consistent theoretical description of πN → e⁺e⁻N and γN → e⁺e⁻N reactions on proton and neutron targets, showing that the resonant structure of the amplitudes generates specific and large quantum interferences between ρ- and ω-meson decays into e⁺e⁻ pairs, but only for total center-of-mass energies √s between 1.50 GeV and 1.75 GeV where the model is applicable.

## Method

The authors computed the πN → e⁺e⁻N and γN → e⁺e⁻N amplitudes using a relativistic, chiral coupled-channel approach that includes πN, πΔ, ρN, ωN, KΛ, KΣ, and ηN hadronic channels, restricted to center-of-mass energies between 1.40 GeV and 1.75 GeV. The vector meson and nucleon in the final state were assumed to be in relative S-wave, with the Bethe-Salpeter kernel constructed from an effective quasi-local meson-meson-baryon-baryon Lagrangian. A generalized Vector Meson Dominance assumption related amplitudes involving photons to those involving vector mesons, with the N(1520), N(1535), N(1650), Δ(1620), and Δ(1700) resonances generated dynamically.

## Key numbers

- The π⁻p → e⁺e⁻n differential cross section at √s = 1.5 GeV is less than 10 nb GeV⁻², showing destructive ρ⁰-ω interference.
- The π⁺n → e⁺e⁻p differential cross section at √s = 1.5 GeV is of the order of 0.15 μb GeV⁻², showing constructive ρ⁰-ω interference.
- The γp → e⁺e⁻p reaction was computed at √s = 1.65 GeV and √s = 1.75 GeV, with vector meson decays dominating over Bethe-Heitler pair production for large e⁺e⁻ pair invariant masses.
- The model is restricted to center-of-mass energies between 1.40 GeV and 1.75 GeV, with the vector meson threshold at √s ≈ 1.72 GeV.

## Context

This work extends the coupled-channel approach of Lutz (2001) to describe dilepton production, providing a consistent framework that connects πN and γN reactions to e⁺e⁻ pair production in the resonance region. The predictions for ρ-ω interference patterns are closely linked to the couplings of the N(1520) and N(1535) resonances to vector meson-nucleon channels, and the γp → e⁺e⁻p reaction is presented as a cleaner process to study ρ-meson photoproduction than γp → π⁻π⁺p, which contains a large Δπ component. Data from the HADES program at GSI and CLAS at JLab are expected to test these predictions and constrain the resonance couplings.
