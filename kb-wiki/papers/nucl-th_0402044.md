# Nucleon resonances and the production of light vector mesons near thresholds

> The production of the light vector mesons $V = ρ, ω, ϕ$ in the reactions $πN \to V N$ and $N N \to V N N$ near threshold is studied. The subsequent electromagnetic decay $V \to γ^* \to e^+ e^-$ is particularly suited for exploring subthreshold $ωN$ resonances.

# Nucleon resonances and the production of light vector mesons near thresholds

## Key claim
The production of light vector mesons (ρ, ω, φ) in πN and NN reactions near threshold can be described using tree-level effective Lagrangians without necessarily requiring an anomalously large hidden strangeness component in the nucleon, though the role of baryon resonances in these processes remains an open question that the authors highlight but do not fully resolve.

## Method
The authors employ tree-level effective Lagrangian models to calculate cross sections for πN → VN and NN → VNN reactions, including s-, u-, and t-channel diagrams with resonances N* and Δ* up to 1720 MeV. They analyze isoscalar-isovector (ρ-ω) interference near the ω threshold, combined ω and φ production with respect to the OZI rule, and compare two different parameter sets for φ meson production to illustrate ambiguities in the effective Lagrangian approach.

## Key numbers
- Resonances N* and Δ* are included up to 1720 MeV in the s-u-channel model for πN → e+e−N reactions
- Angular distribution data for pp → φpp is presented at an excess energy of 83 MeV
- Angular distribution data for pp → ωpp is presented at an excess energy of 170 MeV
- The model describes the energy dependence of the total cross section for ω production in a "fairly large range" (no specific numerical range given in the text)

## Context
This work extends earlier coupled-channel analyses and effective field theory approaches to baryon resonance dynamics, building on the proposal by Madeleine (no year given) for using πN → e+e−N as a probe of resonance dynamics. The authors compare their results with the more sophisticated approach of Madeleine (no year given) and with photo-production studies by Oh (no year given). The calculations are motivated by experiments with the HADES detector at GSI and COSY-ANKE, and the parameterizations are intended to serve as input for calculations of reactions such as pd → dφp_spec and pd → dωp_spec.
