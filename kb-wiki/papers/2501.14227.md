# Theoretical study of the $ΣN$ cusp in the $K^-d\rightarrowπΛN$ reaction

> The $K^-d\rightarrowπΛN$ reaction is useful for exploring the hyperon-nucleon interaction through final state interactions. In particular, the cusp structure of the $ΛN$ invariant mass spectrum at the $ΣN$ threshold contains information about the s-wave interaction of 1/2-isospin hyperon-nucleon systems. The calculation of the spectrum is performed with the aim of extracting the scattering length of the $ΣN(I=1/2)$ channel that couples to the $ΛN$ channel from this reaction, and the results are discussed in comparison with experimental data to highlight the factors that should be considered.

# Theoretical study of the ΣN cusp in the K⁻d → πΛN reaction

## Key claim
The shape of the ΛN invariant mass spectrum near the ΣN threshold in the K⁻d → πΛN reaction is sensitive to the spin-triplet ΣN(I=1/2) scattering length, but extracting this parameter requires careful treatment of background processes and high-resolution experimental data, as the cusp structure is also influenced by the effective range, higher partial waves, and the splitting of the ΣN threshold into Σ⁺n and Σ⁰p states.

## Method
The authors calculate the ΛN invariant mass spectrum by factorizing the scattering amplitude into a two-step process: a first-step K⁻N → πΣ meson-baryon amplitude (using partial-wave amplitudes up to p-wave from Jido (2002)) and a second-step ΣN → ΛN hyperon-nucleon amplitude. The hyperon-nucleon amplitude is constructed from a real symmetric kernel V and momentum matrix P based on S-matrix unitarity, with the constant matrix V fixed to reproduce the empirical Λp spin-triplet scattering length and three different theoretical ΣN(I=1/2) scattering lengths from the NSC97f, Jülich'04, and SMS N²LO(600) potentials. Background processes including Λ exchange, one-step K⁻N → πΛ conversion, and meson exchange diagrams are also included, and kinematic cuts of cosθ_π > 0.9 and proton momentum > 150 MeV/c are applied to suppress backgrounds.

## Key numbers
- The empirical Λp spin-triplet scattering length used to fix the model is a^t_Λp = -1.56^{+0.19}_{-0.22} fm, from Budzanowski (2010).
- Three parameter sets for the spin-triplet ΣN(I=1/2) scattering length are used: Param. 1 = 1.68 - 2.35i fm (from NSC97f, Rijken 1999), Param. 2 = -3.83 - 3.01i fm (from Jülich'04, Haidenbauer 2005), and Param. 3 = 2.53 - 2.64i fm (from SMS N²LO(600), Haidenbauer 2023).
- The corresponding conversion scattering lengths a^t_ΣN→ΛN at the ΣN threshold are: Param. 1 = 1.28 - 0.03i fm, Param. 2 = 0.24 - 1.43i fm, and Param. 3 = 1.33 + 0.25i fm.
- The two ΣN thresholds are at 2128.9 MeV (Σ⁺n) and 2130.8 MeV (Σ⁰p).
- The calculation uses an incident kaon momentum of 760 MeV/c for comparison with experimental data from Braun (1977), with an experimental incident flux of 7.1 counts/μb.

## Context
This work extends previous studies of the ΣN cusp phenomenon in the K⁻d → π⁻Λp reaction, including the analysis by Dalitz (1981) and experimental observations with in-flight kaons by Braun (1977). It is motivated by the ongoing J-PARC E90 experiment (Ichikawa 2022), which is expected to provide higher resolution and statistics than previously available. The results enable the possibility of indirectly measuring the spin-triplet ΣN(I=1/2) scattering length through comparison with high-resolution experimental data, which would complement direct scattering measurements from CLAS (Rowley 2021) and J-PARC E40 (Miwa 2021), as well as femtoscopic studies from ALICE (2022).
