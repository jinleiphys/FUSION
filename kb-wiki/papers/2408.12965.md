# Search for the $Σ(1380)1/2^-$ state in $Λ^+_c \to γπ^+ Λ$ decay by triangle singularity

> In this work, we investigate the resonance production in $Λ^+_c \to γπ^+ Λ$ decay through the triangle singularity (TS) mechanism, within an effective Lagrangian approach. We find that the appropriate loop decay process could develop a triangle singularity in the invariant mass $M_{πΛ}$ around $1.35$ GeV, with the shape depending on the quantum numbers of $Σ^*$ states that couple to the final $πΛ$ system. Especially, the $Σ(1380)1/2^-$ state newly predicted in the pentaquark model, if exists, significantly enhances the TS contribution and sharpens the TS peak due to the $S$-wave $Σ^*πΣ$ vertex in the loop. Therefore, the existence of the $Σ(1380)1/2^-$ state can be examined by measuring the TS signal in $Λ^+_c \to γπ^+ Λ$. Considering also possible tree-level diagrams, we additionally obtain the branching ratio ${\rm Br}(Λ^+_c \to γπ^+ Λ)\simeq 1.5\times 10^{-5}$. We suggest the investigation of this reaction by future BESIII, LHCb, and Belle experiments.

# Search for the Σ(1380)1/2⁻ state in Λ⁺_c → γπ⁺Λ decay by triangle singularity

## Key claim

The existence of the Σ(1380)1/2⁻ state, predicted by pentaquark models, can be inferred from a sharp triangle singularity (TS) peak in the πΛ invariant mass spectrum of Λ⁺_c → γπ⁺Λ decay around 1.35 GeV, rather than from a conventional Breit-Wigner resonance peak. The claim is conditional on the Σ(1380)1/2⁻ having S-wave couplings to πΣ and πΛ, which enhance the TS contribution relative to the P-wave couplings of the known Σ(1385)3/2⁺ state.

## Method

The authors employ an effective Lagrangian approach to compute the triangle loop diagram for Λ⁺_c → γπ⁺Λ, involving intermediate ω, Σ⁺, and π⁰ particles that couple to Σ* resonances (Σ(1380)1/2⁻ or Σ(1385)3/2⁺) decaying to π⁺Λ. They calculate the loop integrals numerically using LoopTools and include tree-level diagrams (Σ⁰ and ρ⁺ exchange) with an adjustable phase φ to account for interference effects. The differential decay width dΓ/dM_{πΛ} is computed for two scenarios: with and without the Σ(1380)1/2⁻ contribution.

## Key numbers

- The triangle singularity occurs at M_{πΛ} = 1.348 GeV when using nominal masses from the Particle Data Group.
- The Σ(1380)1/2⁻ mass is taken as 1380 MeV and its total width as 120 MeV, following Wu et al. (2009) and Zou et al. (2010).
- The branching ratio Br(Λ⁺_c → γπ⁺Λ) is approximately 1.5 × 10⁻⁵, with values ranging from 1.22 × 10⁻⁵ to 1.90 × 10⁻⁵ depending on the phase φ between TS and tree-level amplitudes.
- The coupling constant for the Λ⁺_c → Σ⁺ω weak decay vertex is taken as g_{Λ_cΣω} = 4.70 × 10⁻⁷.
- The Σ(1380)1/2⁻ branching ratios are assumed as Br(πΛ) = 0.870 and Br(πΣ) = 0.117, yielding couplings g_{Σ*₁πΛ} = 1.98 and g_{Σ*₁πΣ} = 0.64.

## Context

This work extends previous studies of the Σ(1380)1/2⁻ state predicted by pentaquark models (Zhang 2004, Helminen 2000) and builds on triangle singularity mechanisms used to explain anomalies in J/ψ decays (Wu 2011, Aceti 2012) and to determine the X(3872) mass (Guo 2019, Sakai 2020). The authors specifically follow the approach of Huang (2024) for Λ⁺_c → ηπ⁺Λ and apply it to the radiative decay channel. The reaction Λ⁺_c → γπ⁺Λ is suggested for experimental investigation at BESIII, LHCb, and Belle, where the sharp TS peak at M_{πΛ} ≈ 1.35 GeV would serve as a distinctive signature of the Σ(1380)1/2⁻ state, distinguishable from the broader Σ(1385)3/2⁺ contribution.
