# Phenomenological model of the Kaonic Nuclear Cluster K^-pp in the ground state

> A phenomenological model is proposed for the analysis of the properties of the Kaonic Nuclear Cluster (KNC) Kpp in its ground state. Inside the KNC K^-pp we describe the relative motion of the protons in the pp pair and the motion of the K^- meson relative to the pp pair in terms of wave functions of 3-dimensional harmonic oscillators. The interaction strength is parameterised in our model by the frequency of the longitudinal oscillation of the K^- meson relative to the pp pair. This parameter was determined with the binding energy and width of the strange baryon Lambda(1405) by assuming that it is a bound state of the K^-p pair. In terms of this interaction strength we calculate the binding energy epsilon_(Kpp) = - 118 MeV, the partial widths of non-pionic decay channels and the total width, Gamma_(Kpp) = 58 MeV. This agrees well with the experimental data by the FINUDA Collaboration (PRL 94, 212303 (2005)): epsilon_exp = - 115(+6/-5) MeV and Gamma_exp = 67(+14/-11) MeV. The discrepancy with the results, obtained by Akaishi and Yamazaki within the potential model approach, is discussed.

## Key claim

The paper proposes a phenomenological oscillator model for the kaonic nuclear cluster K^-pp (denoted ^2_¯KH) that yields a binding energy of -118 MeV and a total width of 58 MeV, which agrees with the FINUDA experimental values of -115(+6/-5) MeV and 67(+14/-11) MeV, but the model relies on the assumption that the interaction strength parameter ω = 64 MeV can be fixed by treating the Λ(1405) baryon as a bound K^-p state with the same stiffness as the K^-pp system.

## Method

The authors model the K^-pp system as a symmetrical linear triatomic molecule, describing the relative motion of the protons in the pp pair and the motion of the K^- meson relative to the pp pair using three-dimensional harmonic oscillator wave functions in momentum representation. The interaction strength is parameterized by a single frequency ω, determined by fitting the binding energy and width of the Λ(1405) resonance (treated as a K^-p bound state) using the Weinberg-Tomozawa term from chiral Lagrangians to leading order in the large N_C expansion. The binding energy and partial decay widths of the K^-pp system are then calculated as momentum integrals of the S-wave elastic K^-pp scattering amplitude weighted with the oscillator wave functions.

## Key numbers

- Binding energy of K^-pp: ε_(^2_¯KH) = -118 MeV, compared to FINUDA experimental value ε_exp = -115(+6/-5) MeV
- Total width of K^-pp: Γ_(^2_¯KH) = 58 MeV, compared to FINUDA experimental value Γ_exp = 67(+14/-11) MeV
- Interaction strength parameter: ω = 64 MeV, fixed using Λ(1405) mass 1406 ± 4 MeV and width 50 ± 2 MeV
- Partial width for decay ^2_¯KH → pΛ^0: 34 MeV
- Partial width for decay ^2_¯KH → pΣ^0: 2 MeV
- Partial width for decay ^2_¯KH → nΣ^+: 22 MeV
- Ratio of intensities I_(pΛ^0) : I_(pΣ^0) = 17 : 1
- Reduced mass of K^-pp system: μ = 391 MeV, using m_K = 494 MeV and m_N = 940 MeV

## Context

This work extends the investigation of kaonic nuclear clusters first studied by Akaishi and Yamazaki within a non-relativistic potential model approach, who predicted a binding energy of -48 MeV and a non-pionic width of about 12 MeV for K^-pp. The present model achieves better agreement with the FINUDA Collaboration (2005) experimental data by using a different phenomenological approach based on harmonic oscillator wave functions and chiral Lagrangian techniques. The treatment of Λ(1405) as a K^-p quasi-bound state is consistent with coupled-channels analyses by Weise and collaborators (1995, 1998) that generated this resonance dynamically.
