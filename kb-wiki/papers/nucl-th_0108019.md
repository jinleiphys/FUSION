# Isoscalar-Isovector Interferences in $πN \to N e^+ e^-$ Reactions as a Probe of Baryon Resonance Dynamics

> The isoscalar-isovector ($ρ-ω$) interferences in the exclusive reactions $π^- p \to n e^+ e^-$ and $π^+ n \to p e^+ e^-$ near the $ω$ threshold leads to a distinct difference of the dielectron invariant mass distributions depending on beam energy. The strength of this effect is determined by the coupling of resonances to the nucleon vector-meson channels and other resonance properties. Therefore, a combined analysis of these reactions can be used as a tool for determining the baryon resonance dynamics.

## Key claim

The isoscalar-isovector (rho-omega) interference in the exclusive reactions π⁻p → n e⁺e⁻ and π⁺n → p e⁺e⁻ near the ω threshold produces a distinct difference in dielectron invariant mass distributions that depends on beam energy, and this effect can serve as a tool for probing baryon resonance dynamics, provided the coupling constants and phases of the resonances are properly constrained.

## Method

The authors calculated differential cross sections for dielectron production in πN → N e⁺e⁻ reactions using a resonance model with s- and u-channel diagrams and effective Lagrangians, including nucleon and baryon resonances with masses up to 1.72 GeV (N, N*, Δ states). They employed coupling constants from the quark model of Riska and Brown (2000) and, alternatively, adjusted ρNB* couplings from experimental resonance decay branching ratios, then compared the resulting invariant mass distributions and angular distributions for the two charge-conjugate reactions.

## Key numbers

- ω meson total decay width: Γ_ω = 8.41 MeV (from PDG 1998)
- ρ meson width at pole: Γ_ρ⁰ = 150.7 MeV (from PDG 1998)
- ρ → e⁺e⁻ decay width: Γ_{ρ→e⁺e⁻} = 6.77 keV, giving f_ρ = 0.06
- ω → e⁺e⁻ decay width: Γ_{ω→e⁺e⁻} = 0.60 keV, giving f_ω = 0.0177
- ωNN coupling: g_{ωNN} = 10.35, κ_{ωNN} = 0 (from Riska and Brown 2000)
- ρNN coupling: g_{ρNN} = 3, κ_{ρNN} = 6.1 (from Riska and Brown 2000)
- πNN coupling: f_{πNN} = 1.0
- Form factor cutoff: Λ_B = 0.66 GeV (fitted to total ω production cross section near threshold)
- The difference in cross section between π⁻p and π⁺n reactions reaches a factor up to three, depending on energy and invariant mass
- Calculations performed at center-of-mass energies s^{1/2} = 1.6 and 1.8 GeV, and at dielectron production angle θ = 30°

## Context

This work extends the analysis of ω production in πN interactions by Titov, Kampfer, and Reznik (2001) and the earlier discussion of ρ-ω interference in π⁻p → n e⁺e⁻ by Schumacher et al. (2000). It provides predictions testable with the HADES spectrometer at GSI/Darmstadt, which can use pion beams to measure dielectron production. The approach relies on the resonance model of Riska and Brown (2000) for coupling constants and phases, and contrasts with coupled-channel analyses by Lutz, Wolf, and Friman (1999) and by Manley and Pennington (2000) that aim to extract couplings from combined reaction data. The proposed combined analysis of π⁺n and π⁻p reactions offers a new method to study the isoscalar part of the electromagnetic current in the baryon resonance region.
