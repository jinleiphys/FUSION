# Role of the $Λ^+_c(2940)$ in the $π^- p \to D^- D^0 p$ reaction close to threshold

> We report on a theoretical study of the $π^- p \to D^- D^0 p$ reaction near threshold within an effective Lagrangian approach. The production process is described by $t$ channel $D^{*0}$ meson exchange, $s$-channel nucleon pole, and $u$ channel $Σ^{++}_{c}$ exchange. In our work, the final $D^0 p$ results from the ground $Λ^+_c(2286)$ state and also dominantly from the excited $Λ^+_c(2940)$ state which is assumed as a $D^{*0} p$ molecular state with spin parity $J^P = \frac{1}{2}^+$ or $\frac{1}{2}^-$. We calculate the total cross section of the $π^- p \to D^- D^0 p$ reaction. It is shown that the spin parity assignment of $\frac{1}{2}^-$ for $Λ^+_c(2940)$ gives a sizable enhancement for the total cross section in comparison with a choice of $J^p = \frac{1}{2}^+$. However, our theoretical result of the total cross section is sensitive to the value of the cutoff parameter involved in the form factor of the exchanged off-shell particles. Moreover, we also calculate the second order differential cross section and find it can be used to determine the parity of the $Λ^+_c(2940)$. It is expected that our model calculations can be tested by future experiments at J-PARC in Japan.

## Key claim

The paper claims that the second-order differential cross section of the reaction π⁻ p → D⁻ D⁰ p can distinguish between the Jᴾ = ½⁺ and Jᴾ = ½⁻ spin-parity assignments for the Λ⁺_c(2940) resonance, but the total cross section alone cannot because it is sensitive to the cutoff parameter in the form factor.

## Method

The authors employed an effective Lagrangian approach with tree-level Feynman diagrams including t-channel D*⁰ meson exchange, s-channel nucleon pole, and u-channel Σ⁺⁺_c exchange. The final D⁰ p system was produced via intermediate Λ⁺_c(2286) and Λ⁺_c(2940) states, with the latter treated as a D*⁰ p molecular state. They calculated total cross sections and second-order differential cross sections for both Jᴾ = ½⁺ and Jᴾ = ½⁻ assignments using coupling constants determined from SU(4) symmetry and a hadronic molecular approach.

## Key numbers

- Mass of Λ⁺_c(2940): 2939.3⁺¹·⁴₋₁·₅ MeV, with total decay width Γ = 17.5 ± 5.2 ± 5.9 MeV (BABAR collaboration)
- D*⁰ p threshold: 2945.2 MeV
- Coupling constants used: g_{D N Σ_c} = -2.69, g_{Λ_c p D} = -13.98, g_{Λ_c p D*} = -5.20, g_{Λ_c π Σ_c} = 9.32, g_{D* D π} = 14.1
- For Λ⁺_c(2940) as Jᴾ = ½⁺: g_{Λ*_c p D*} = 6.64, g_{Λ*_c p D} = -0.54
- For Λ⁺_c(2940) as Jᴾ = ½⁻: f_{Λ*_c p D*} = 3.75, f_{Λ*_c p D} = -0.97
- Total cross section reduces by a factor of 10 when cutoff Λ decreases from 3 GeV to 2.5 GeV
- Changing spin-parity from ½⁺ to ½⁻ enhances total cross section by a factor of more than 10
- Sharp growth in total cross section occurs around beam momentum p_{π⁻} = 12 GeV, where the invariant mass of the D⁰ p system reaches 2.94 GeV

## Context

This work extends previous studies of Λ⁺_c(2940) production in p p̅ → p D⁰ Λ̅_c(2286) reactions by He (2011) and Dong (2014), which did not include initial state interactions or D* meson exchange. The present study uses pion-induced reactions relevant to J-PARC experiments, where pion energies above 20 GeV are available. The results enable future experiments at J-PARC to determine the parity of the Λ⁺_c(2940) resonance through measurement of the second-order differential cross section, which is less sensitive to the cutoff parameter than the total cross section.
