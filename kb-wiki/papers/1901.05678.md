# $ΩNN$ and $ΩΩN$ states

> The lattice QCD analysis of the HAL QCD Collaboration has recently derived $ΩN$ and $ΩΩ$ interacting potentials with nearly physical quark masses ($m_π\simeq $ 146 MeV and $m_K \simeq $ 525 MeV). They found an attractive interaction in the $ΩN$ $^5S_2$ channel which supports a bound state with a central binding energy of 1.54 MeV. The $ΩΩ$ $^1S_0$ channel shows an overall attraction with a bound state with a central binding energy of 1.6 MeV. In this paper we looked closely at the $ΩNN$ and $ΩΩN$ three-body systems making use of the latest HAL QCD Collaboration $ΩN$ and $ΩΩ$ interactions. Our results show that the $Ωd$ system in the state with maximal spin $(I)J^P=(0)5/2^+$ is bound with a binding energy of about 20 MeV. The $(I)J^P=(1)3/2^+$ $Ωnn$ state presents a resonance decaying to $ΛΞn$ and $ΣΞn$, with a separation energy of $\sim$ 1 MeV. The $(I)J^P=(1/2)1/2^+$ $ΩΩN$ state also exhibits a resonance decaying to $ΛΞΩ$ and $ΣΞΩ$, with a separation energy of $\sim$ 4.6 MeV. We have calculated the contribution of the Coulomb potential to differentiate among the different charged states.

# ΩNN and ΩΩN states

## Key claim
Using the latest HAL QCD Collaboration ΩN and ΩΩ interactions derived at nearly physical quark masses (mπ ≈ 146 MeV, mK ≈ 525 MeV), the authors find that the Ωd system in the maximal spin channel (I)J^P=(0)5/2^+ is bound with about 20 MeV, while the Ωnn and ΩΩN states appear as resonances decaying to lower channels, but the widths remain uncalculated because the necessary transition potentials to inelastic channels have not yet been derived from lattice QCD.

## Method
The authors solved the three-body bound-state Faddeev equations for systems with two identical particles (ΩNN and ΩΩN), restricting all three baryons to S-wave states. The two-body t-matrices were obtained by solving the Lippmann-Schwinger equation using the HAL QCD Collaboration's parametrized ΩN and ΩΩ potentials, together with a Malfliet-Tjon NN potential for the nucleon-nucleon subsystem. The Coulomb potential was included as a screened Yukawa term to separate binding energies of different charged states.

## Key numbers
- ΩN ^5S_2 bound state central binding energy: 1.54 MeV (from HAL QCD, used as input)
- ΩΩ ^1S_0 bound state central binding energy: 1.6 MeV (from HAL QCD, used as input)
- Ωd (I)J^P=(0)5/2^+ binding energy: about 20 MeV (range 19.6–20.0 MeV across four ΩN potential models, using experimental masses)
- Ωnn (I)J^P=(1)3/2^+ separation energy: ∼1 MeV (range 0.85–0.97 MeV across models)
- ΩΩN (I)J^P=(1/2)1/2^+ separation energy: ∼4.6 MeV (range 4.5–4.8 MeV across models)
- Coulomb correction for Ωp vs Ωn: 0.90–0.92 MeV, consistent with HAL QCD's 0.92 MeV
- Coulomb extra binding for Ωpp vs Ωnn: 0.7 MeV
- Coulomb extra binding for Ωd: about 1.3 MeV
- Coulomb reduces ΩΩ binding from 1.6 MeV to 0.9 MeV
- Coulomb splitting between ΩΩp and ΩΩn: 1.3 MeV

## Context
This work extends the authors' previous study (Garcilazo and Valcarce, 2018) of the Ωd system using preliminary HAL QCD potentials, where a binding energy of about 17 MeV was found. The present results use the final HAL QCD interactions (Iritani et al., 2018; Gongyo et al., 2018) at nearly physical quark masses, which give stronger ΩN attraction (1.54 MeV binding vs 0.3 MeV in the preliminary analysis). The study is motivated by the empirical pattern in the nonstrange sector where binding per baryon increases with baryon number, and by the recent STAR measurement of the proton-Ω correlation function favoring a bound state hypothesis. The results suggest that these three-body states could be observed at J-PARC, FAIR, RHIC, or LHC experiments, though the authors note that coupled-channel calculations of the resonance widths require transition potentials not yet available from lattice QCD.
