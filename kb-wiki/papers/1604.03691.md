# Nondiffractive feature of $γN\to ρ^\pm N$ with $ρ$-meson electromagnetic multipole moments

> We investigate photoproduction of charged $ρ$ off the nucleon using $ρ(770)+π(140)$ Regge pole exchanges by considering the $ρ$-meson electromagnetic multipole moments. The significance of the Ward identity at the $γρρ$ vertex is emphasized for current conservation in the process. Given $π$ exchange with the well-known coupling constants for $γπρ$ and $πNN$, we analyze the role of the $ρ$ exchange in the $γp\to ρ^+n$ and $γn\toρ^-p$ processes without model-dependences except for the magnetic moment $μ_{ρ^\pm}=\pm2.01$ and electric quadrupole moment ${\cal Q}_{ρ^\pm}=\pm0.027$ fm$^2$ taken from theoretical estimates. The nondiffractive feature of both cross sections is reproduced with a rapid decrease beyond the resonance region by the dominance of $π$ exchange over the $ρ$\,. % Cross sections for differential and density matrix elements are presented to compare with existing data. The parity and photon polarization asymmetries are predicted to demonstrate the apparent roles of the $ρ$-meson electromagnetic multipole moments.

# Nondiffractive feature of γN → ρ±N with ρ-meson electromagnetic multipole moments

## Key claim
The nondiffractive rapid decrease of the charged ρ photoproduction cross sections beyond the resonance region is reproduced by π exchange dominating over ρ exchange, but only if exchange degeneracy (EXD) between the ρ and a2 trajectories is broken by assigning the canonical phase to ρ, rather than using the usual EXD phase or a cutoff function.

## Method
The authors constructed a Regge-pole model for γp → ρ+n and γn → ρ-p using ρ(770) and π(140) exchanges in the t-channel, with the γρρ vertex constrained by the Ward identity to ensure current conservation. The ρ exchange amplitude was gauge-invariant by including nucleon pole terms and a contact term, while the π exchange used known coupling constants from the ρ± → πγ decay width (68 keV) and πNN coupling. The ρ trajectory was taken as αρ(t) = 0.9(t - mρ²) + 1 and the π trajectory as απ(t) = 0.7(t - mπ²), with the ρ exchange assigned the canonical phase ½(-1 + e^{-iπαρ(t)}) rather than the EXD phase.

## Key numbers
- Magnetic moment of charged ρ meson: μρ± = ±2.01 (in units of e/2mρ)
- Electric quadrupole moment of charged ρ meson: Qρ± = ±0.027 fm²
- Coupling constant gγπρ = 0.224 (magnitude), estimated from Γ(ρ± → πγ) = 68 keV
- πNN coupling constant: gπNN = 13.4
- ρNN vector coupling: gρv = 2.6; tensor coupling: gρt = 9.62 (ratio gρt/gρv = 3.7)
- ρ trajectory intercept: αρ(0) = 0.35 (when using αρ(t) = 0.8t + 0.35 for the smaller-intercept case)
- Maximum total cross section for γn → ρ-p: σmax ≈ 7 μb at Eγ ≈ 1.6 GeV
- Charge asymmetry measured on deuteron: (σγd→ρ+m - σγd→ρ-m)/(σγd→ρ+m + σγd→ρ-m) ≈ -0.11 ± 0.03, giving σρ-/σρ+ ≈ 1.25
- Energy range of LAMP2 data for γp → ρ+n: Eγ = 2.8 to 4.8 GeV
- Energy of Rochester-Cornell data for γp → ρ+n: Eγ = 9.6 GeV
- Energy range of ABBHHM data for γn → ρ-p: Eγ = 1 to 5 GeV
- Dipped structure in differential cross section for ρ exchange at -t ≈ 0.5 GeV²/c²

## Context
This work extends the Regge model treatment of charged ρ photoproduction beyond earlier studies by Clark and Donnachie (using EXD breaking) and Laget (using a cutoff function), while incorporating the Ward identity for the γρρ vertex that was not properly satisfied in earlier work by Clark, Joos, and Reka. The authors note that unlike the diffractive ρ⁰ process dominated by Pomeron exchange (Laget, 2001), charged ρ production proceeds via nonresonant meson exchanges. The results enable future experimental determination of the ρ-meson electromagnetic multipole moments through measurements of parity asymmetry Pσ and photon polarization asymmetry Σ, which the authors predict to be sensitive to these moments.
