# Energy level displacement of excited np states of kaonic hydrogen

> We compute the energy level displacement of the excited np states of kaonic hydrogen within the quantum field theoretic and relativistic covariant model of strong low-energy bar-KN interactions suggested in EPJA21, 11 (2004). For the width of the energy level of the excited 2p state of kaonic hydrogen, caused by strong low-energy interactions, we find Gamma_2p = 2 meV. This result is important for the theoretical analysis of the X-ray yields in kaonic hydrogen.

## Key claim
The paper computes the energy level displacement of excited np states of kaonic hydrogen using a quantum field theoretic and relativistic covariant model of strong low-energy antikaon-nucleon interactions, finding a width for the 2p state of 2 meV. The abstract glosses over the fact that this result depends on a specific model incorporating strange resonances (Lambda(1405), Sigma(1750)) and exotic scalar states (a0(980), f0(980)) treated as elementary particles with phenomenological coupling constants, and that the soft-kaon theorem contribution to the P-wave scattering length is negligible compared to resonance contributions.

## Method
The authors extended their earlier model of S-wave K^-p scattering to P-wave states by computing P-wave scattering lengths for elastic K^-p scattering and inelastic reactions K^-p → Yπ (Yπ = Σ^+π^-, Σ^-π^+, Σ^0π^0, Λ^0π^0) with total angular momentum J = 1/2 and J = 3/2. They included contributions from baryon resonances (N(1440), Λ(1600), Σ(1660), N(1710), Λ(1810), Σ(1880) for J=1/2; Δ(1232), Σ(1385), N(1720), Λ(1890), Σ(1840) for J=3/2), an elastic background from current algebra and soft-kaon theorems, and exchanges of exotic scalar mesons a0(980) and f0(980). The energy level shift and width for np states were then derived using these scattering lengths in formulas involving the kaonic hydrogen wave functions.

## Key numbers
- Width of the excited 2p state of kaonic hydrogen: Γ_{2p} = 2 meV = 3 × 10^{12} sec^{-1}
- Bohr radius of kaonic hydrogen: a_B = 83 fm (using reduced mass μ = 324 MeV and fine-structure constant α = 1/137.036)
- Resonance contribution to P-wave scattering length a^{K^-p}_{1/2}: sum_R (a^{K^-p}_{1/2})_R = -0.013 m^{-3}_π
- Exotic scalar meson contribution to a^{K^-p}_{1/2}: a^{K^-p}_{1/2,K\bar{K}} = -0.018 m^{-3}_π
- Current algebra contribution to a^{K^-p}_{1/2}: (a^{K^-p}_{1/2})_{CA} = (a^{K^-p}_{1/2})_{SKT} + 0.024 m^{-3}_π
- Total P-wave scattering length combination: 2 a^{K^-p}_{3/2} + a^{K^-p}_{1/2} = (2 a^{K^-p}_{3/2} + a^{K^-p}_{1/2})_{SKT} + 0.111 m^{-3}_π
- Soft-kaon theorem contribution: (2 a^{K^-p}_{3/2} + a^{K^-p}_{1/2})_{SKT} = 0.002 m^{-3}_π
- Resonance contribution to a^{K^-p}_{3/2} from Σ(1385): (a^{K^-p}_{3/2})_{Σ^0_3} = 0.060 m^{-3}_π
- Resonance contribution to a^{K^-p}_{3/2} from Λ(1890) and Σ(1840): sum_{R = Λ^0_4,Σ^0_4} (a^{K^-p}_{3/2})_R = -0.001 m^{-3}_π

## Context
This work extends the authors' earlier model of kaonic hydrogen ground state properties (Ivanov et al., 2004, EPJA 21, 11) to excited np states, providing the first calculation of the 2p state width within this framework. The result is important for analyzing X-ray yields from kaonic hydrogen measured by the DEAR Collaboration, as the hadronic decay rate from excited states affects the accuracy of ground state measurements. The approach contrasts with the effective field theory method of Meissner, Raha, and Rusetsky (2004), who used chiral perturbation theory for ns states, and enables comparison with future experimental data on excited state transitions.
