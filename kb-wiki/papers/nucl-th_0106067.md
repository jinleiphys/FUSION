# Heavy Quarkonium Dissociation Cross Sections in Relativistic Heavy-Ion Collisions

> Many of the hadron-hadron cross sections required for the study of the dynamics of matter produced in relativistic heavy-ion collisions can be calculated using the quark-interchange model. Here we evaluate the low-energy dissociation cross sections of $J/ψ$, $ψ'$, $χ$, $Υ$, and $Υ'$ in collision with $π$, $ρ$, and $K$, which are important for the interpretation of heavy-quarkonium suppression as a signature for the quark gluon plasma. These comover dissociation processes also contribute to heavy-quarkonium suppression, and must be understood and incorporated in simulations of heavy-ion collisions before QGP formation can be established through this signature.

## Key claim

The quark-interchange model can be used to calculate low-energy dissociation cross sections of heavy quarkonia by light mesons, but these cross sections must be understood and incorporated into simulations of heavy-ion collisions before quark-gluon plasma formation can be established through heavy-quarkonium suppression as a signature.

## Method

The authors employed the Barnes-Swanson quark-interchange model, using nonrelativistic quark potential model wavefunctions and interquark interactions (color-Coulomb, linear confining, and spin-spin hyperfine terms) to evaluate Born-order scattering amplitudes for meson-meson rearrangement reactions. They calculated the spatial overlap integrals by expanding meson wavefunctions as linear combinations of Gaussian basis functions, and evaluated spin matrix elements using Wigner 9-j symbols. The cross sections were obtained by integrating the differential cross section over the invariant momentum transfer t, with the post and prior formalisms checked for numerical consistency.

## Key numbers

- For π+J/ψ dissociation, the cross section reaches a maximum of about 7 mb at a center-of-mass kinetic energy of about 0.85 GeV (comparing to Martins et al. (1995) who found a similar result)
- The J/ψ+N cross section at √s=5 GeV from Kharzeev and Satz (1994) is only about 0.25 μb, with a finite-mass correction increasing this by about a factor of two
- Matinyan and Müller (1998) found J/ψ dissociation cross sections by π and ρ are about 0.2-0.3 mb at √s=4 GeV, with form factors (Gaussian width set to 1.5 GeV) reducing these by an order of magnitude

## Context

This work extends the quark-interchange model previously applied successfully to I=2 ππ scattering (Barnes and Swanson 1992), I=3/2 Kπ scattering, and S-wave KN scattering. It contrasts with earlier approaches using perturbative QCD (Kharzeev and Satz 1994) which gave very small cross sections, and with effective meson Lagrangian models (Matinyan and Muller 1998, Haglin 2000, Lin and Ko 2000, Oh et al. 2000) which gave widely varying results sensitive to form factor choices. The calculations enable more reliable incorporation of comover dissociation processes into simulations of heavy-ion collisions at RHIC and LHC, and are particularly important for interpreting the anomalous J/ψ suppression observed by the NA50 Collaboration in Pb+Pb collisions.
