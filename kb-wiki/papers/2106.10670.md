# Semileptonic decays $D\toηπe^+ν_e$ in the $a_0(980)$ region

> The mechanism of the four-quark production of the light scalar isovector four-quark state $a_0(980)$ in the $D\toηπe^+ν_e$ decays is discussed. It is shown that the characteristic features of the shape of the $ηπ$ mass spectra expected in our scheme can serve as the indicator of the production mechanism and internal structure of the $a_0(980)$ resonance.

## Key claim

The characteristic shape of the ηπ mass spectrum in D → ηπ e⁺ ν_e decays can serve as an indicator of the four-quark production mechanism and internal structure of the a₀(980) resonance, but the model's predictions depend on the choice of the subtraction constant C_{K⁰K⁻} and the product f⁺(0)g₀, which determine the spectrum's normalization and shape.

## Method

The authors construct a model for the semileptonic decays D⁰ → ηπ⁻ e⁺ ν_e and D⁺ → ηπ⁰ e⁺ ν_e where the a₀(980) resonance is produced via OZI-allowed four-quark fluctuations of the dū or d\bar d source (dū → d\bar q q ū, etc.), which generate ηπ, η'π, and K\bar K intermediate states. These seed fluctuations are dressed by strong final-state interactions using dispersion relations, with the a₀(980) amplitude expressed through loop integrals I_{ab}(s) and the resonance propagator D_{a₀}(s). The model contains two free parameters: the normalization product f⁺(0)g₀ and the subtraction constant C_{K⁰K⁻}, while the a₀(980) mass and coupling are fixed at m_{a₀} = 0.985 GeV and g²_{a₀ηπ}/(16π) = 0.2 GeV².

## Key numbers

- BESIII measured branching fractions: B(D⁰ → a₀(980)⁻ e⁺ ν_e) × B(a₀(980)⁻ → ηπ⁻) = [1.33^{+0.33}_{-0.29}(stat) ± 0.09(syst)] × 10⁻⁴
- BESIII measured branching fractions: B(D⁺ → a₀(980)⁰ e⁺ ν_e) × B(a₀(980)⁰ → ηπ⁰) = [1.66^{+0.81}_{-0.66}(stat) ± 0.11(syst)] × 10⁻⁴
- BESIII measured ratio: Γ(D⁰ → a₀(980)⁻ e⁺ ν_e) / Γ(D⁺ → a₀(980)⁰ e⁺ ν_e) = 2.03 ± 0.95 ± 0.06
- Signal events: 25.7^{+6.4}_{-5.7} for D⁰ → a₀(980)⁻ e⁺ ν_e (6.4σ significance); 10.2^{+5.0}_{-4.1} for D⁺ → a₀(980)⁰ e⁺ ν_e (2.9σ significance)
- a₀(980)⁻ mass used in model: m_{a₀⁻} = 0.985 GeV; coupling g²_{a₀ηπ}/(16π) = 0.2 GeV²
- Decay width Γ_{a₀→ηπ} at √s = m_{a₀} ≈ 132 MeV; visible width of a₀(980) peak at half-maximum in ηπ channel ≈ 55 MeV
- Mixing angles: θ_i = 35.3° (ideal mixing), θ_p = -11.3° (pseudoscalar nonet mixing)
- Form factor pole mass: m_A = m_{D₁⁺} = 2.42 GeV
- CKM matrix element: |V_{cd}| = 0.221 ± 0.004
- BESIII data collected at center-of-mass energy 3.773 GeV (ψ(3770) production)

## Context

This work extends the authors' previous study (Achasov and Kiselev, 2018) of D → ηπ e⁺ ν_e decays, where a₀(980) production was assumed to occur via mixing with the heavy q\bar q state a₀'(1400). The present model instead uses OZI-allowed four-quark fluctuations of the dū and d\bar d sources, similar to the approach used by Achasov et al. (2020, 2021) for D_s⁺ → f₀(980) e⁺ ν_e and D⁺ → f₀(500) e⁺ ν_e decays. The predictions can be tested against BESIII data (2018) on D⁰ → a₀(980)⁻ e⁺ ν_e and D⁺ → a₀(980)⁰ e⁺ ν_e, and future high-statistics experiments are needed to resolve the a₀(980) line shape and distinguish between production mechanisms.
