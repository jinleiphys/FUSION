# Shell model results for nuclear $β^-$-decay properties of $sd$ shell nuclei

> We evaluate the allowed $β^-$-decay properties of nuclei with $Z = 8 - 15$ systematically under the framework of the nuclear shell model with the use of the valence space Hamiltonians derived from modern $ab~intio$ methods, such as in-medium similarity renormalization group and coupled-cluster theory. For comparison we also show results obtained with fitted interaction derived from the chiral effective field theory and phenomenological USDB interaction. We have performed calculations for O $\rightarrow$ F, F $\rightarrow$ Ne, Ne $\rightarrow$ Na, Na $\rightarrow$ Mg, Mg $\rightarrow$ Al, Al $\rightarrow$ Si, Si $\rightarrow$ P and P $\rightarrow$ S transitions. Theoretical results of $B(GT)$, log$ft$ values and half-lives, are discussed and compared with the available experimental data.

## Key claim

The paper systematically evaluates allowed β⁻-decay properties of sd-shell nuclei (Z = 8–15) using shell-model calculations with ab initio valence-space Hamiltonians (IM-SRG and CCEI), a fitted chiral EFT interaction, and the phenomenological USDB interaction, finding that the ab initio and chiral interactions reproduce experimental Gamow-Teller strengths and half-lives with slightly larger root-mean-square deviations than the phenomenological USDB interaction, and that quenching factors for the effective axial-vector coupling gA range from q = 0.62 to 0.77 depending on the interaction.

## Method

Shell-model calculations were performed in the sd model space using the NuShellX code with four interactions: two ab initio interactions (in-medium similarity renormalization group, IM-SRG, and coupled-cluster effective interactions, CCEI) derived from chiral N3LO NN and N2LO 3N forces, a fitted chiral EFT interaction (CEFT) with LECs fitted to 441 ground- and excited-state energies, and the phenomenological USDB interaction. Gamow-Teller strengths B(GT), logft values, and half-lives were computed for β⁻ transitions from O→F, F→Ne, Ne→Na, Na→Mg, Mg→Al, Al→Si, Si→P, and P→S, using experimental Q values from AME2016 and phase-space factors with Wilkinson-Macefield parameters and Sirlin corrections.

## Key numbers

- Quenching factor q = 0.77 ± 0.02 for USDB interaction, consistent with the value q = 0.764 reported by Richter (2012)
- Quenching factor q = 0.75 ± 0.03 for IM-SRG interaction
- Quenching factor q = 0.62 ± 0.03 for CCEI interaction
- Quenching factor q = 0.73 ± 0.04 for CEFT interaction
- RMS deviations: 0.0356 (USDB), 0.0469 (IM-SRG), 0.0440 (CCEI), 0.0541 (CEFT)
- Effective |gA^eff| values: 0.97 (USDB), 0.95 (IM-SRG), 0.92 (CEFT), 0.78 (CCEI), using |gA^free| = 1.26
- For the transition ³⁴Si(0⁺)→³⁴P(1⁺), experimental M(GT) = 1.759 with logft ≥ 3.3, while theoretical values range from 0.232 (CEFT) to 0.845 (CCEI)

## Context

This work extends earlier shell-model studies of β-decay in the sd shell by Brown and Wildenthal (1988) and Wildenthal et al. (1983), which used phenomenological effective interactions, by providing the first comprehensive comparison of β⁻-decay properties using modern ab initio interactions (IM-SRG and CCEI) and a fitted chiral EFT interaction. The results enable systematic evaluation of quenching factors for the axial-vector coupling strength in the sd shell and provide benchmarks for future ab initio calculations of weak nuclear response, relevant for astrophysical applications and neutrinoless double-beta decay studies as discussed by Menendez (2011).
