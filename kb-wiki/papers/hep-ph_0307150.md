# Generalized parton distributions and composite constituent quarks

> An approach is proposed to calculate Generalized Parton Distributions (GPDs) in a Constituent Quark Model (CQM) scenario, considering the constituent quarks as complex systems. The GPDs are obtained from the wave functions of the non relativistic CQM of Isgur and Karl, convoluted with the GPDs of the constituent quarks themselves. The latter are modelled by using the structure functions of the constituent quark, the double distribution representation of GPDs, and a recently proposed phenomenological constituent quark form factor. The present approach permits to access a kinematical range corresponding to both the DGLAP and the ERBL regions, for small values of the momentum transfer and of the skewedness parameter. In this kinematical region, the cross sections relevant to deeply virtual Compton scattering could be estimated by using the obtained GPDs. As an example, the leading twist, unpolarized GPD $H$ has been calculated. Its general relations with the non relativistic definition of the electric form factor and with the leading twist unpolarized quark density are consistently recovered from our expressions.Further natural applications of the proposed approach are addressed.

## Key claim

The authors propose an approach to calculate Generalized Parton Distributions (GPDs) in a Constituent Quark Model (CQM) where constituent quarks are treated as composite systems, but the calculation is limited to small values of the momentum transfer (Δ² ≪ m², where m is the constituent quark mass) and small skewedness parameter ξ, with the full kinematical range deferred to a follow-up study introducing relativity.

## Method

The GPD H_q for the proton is obtained via a convolution formula: the off-forward momentum distribution of constituent quarks (calculated from the non-relativistic Isgur and Karl CQM wave functions) is convolved with the GPD of the constituent quark itself. The constituent quark GPD is modeled using the double distribution representation, with its forward limit fixed by the Altarelli-Cabibbo-Maiani-Petronzio (ACMP) prescription for constituent quark structure functions, and its off-forward behavior determined by a factorized ansatz involving a phenomenological constituent quark form factor. The calculation is performed in the Breit frame, and the approach accesses both the DGLAP region (x ≥ ξ) and the ERBL region (−ξ ≤ x ≤ ξ) for small momentum transfer and skewedness.

## Key numbers

- The hadronic scale is set at μ₀² = 0.34 GeV², determined from the GRV parametrization
- The approach is valid under the conditions ξ² ≪ 1, k² ≪ m², and Δ² ≪ m² (where m is the constituent quark mass)
- The skewedness parameter ξ is kinematically constrained to 0 ≤ ξ ≤ √(−Δ²)/√(4M²−Δ²), where M is the nucleon mass

## Context

This work extends the forward-scattering approach of Scopetta (2001) and Scopetta (2002), which showed that unpolarized and polarized DIS data are consistent with composite constituent quarks, to the off-forward case of GPDs. It generalizes the earlier CQM calculation of GPDs by Scopetta (2003) and Boffi (2003), which treated constituent quarks as point-like, by introducing composite structure for the constituent quarks. The approach enables estimation of cross sections for deeply virtual Compton scattering (DVCS) and allows calculation of sea quark contributions to GPDs, which was not possible with point-like constituent quarks.
